# Advantages of Open-Source Software

## Open-Source Software and its goals

Open-source software means that a software's source code is available to be read, modified and distributed by anyone with means to do so.

### Openness and Safety
By being open to anyone’s changes, software can be improved and altered by a wider range of people with specialized knowledge in ways that closed-source cannot. Its openness is also important in increasing **safety**; the more people have access to certain software, the more people can spot mistakes and vulnerabilities in it. This means that open-source software is vital for *collaboration and innovation* in the field of software development.

### Cost
The most obvious advantage of open-source software is the fact that it is *free to use*. Its financial benefit, however, goes deeper than that. A [study](https://www.hbs.edu/ris/Publication%20Files/24-038_51f8444f-502c-4139-8bf2-56eb4b65c58a.pdf) has found that the demand-side value of open-source software in the economy is worth about **$8.8 trillion**. The cost for companies would be much higher (3,5 times current spending on software) without open-source software. Open-source software is therefore of *crucial importance to the economy* and a vital pillar of the modern technological world.

### Fostering of Open-Source Culture
Open-source software is not merely a pragmatic thing, it is also based on an **idea**, that being one of *freedom and transparency*. Open-source culture builds a community that is prone to collaboration, open exchange and meritocracy. People get to be involved, creative, working together and can make an impact where they see that something could be improved. Instead of simply being economically beneficial like I mentioned in the previous paragraph, it also goes *in opposition to the profit motive* and helps users become a part of something greater than themselves. The community it fosters opposes capture and monetization by big companies, leading to a better internet experience for everyone.
